How to play

Stuck? Type ?, HELP or I don’t know and press Enter.

Talk naturally: “Where am I?”, “Give me a clue”, “Repeat that”, or use these commands:

LOOK
Explore your surroundings.
READ
Read the current Scripture.
HINT
Get a clue.
CROSSREF
See related passages.
TRAVEL
Explore an offered connection.
RETURN
Resume the main story.
LOOM
See discovered connections.
JOURNAL
Review your progress.
DISCOVER [idea]
Record a research proposal.
SPEAK
Hear Scripture aloud.

Use the suggested actions below each passage. You can always ask for help.
